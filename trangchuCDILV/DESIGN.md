## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a tone of calm authority, collective solidarity, and understated achievement. Designed for the CDILV Union & Competition Portal, it bridges two distinct functions: institutional union governance (transparency, member voice, reliable record-keeping) and competitive recognition (podiums, merit, distinction).

The aesthetic direction is **Premium Civic Minimalism**. It combines the structured clarity of civic-grade architecture with airy, editorial restraint. Rather than leaning bureaucratic or aggressively athletic, the interface feels expansive, quiet, and deliberate. High proportions of whitespace and ice-tinted slate backdrops set a cool, balanced ground, allowing elevated white surfaces and refined warm gold accents to highlight member milestones without visual noise.

Interactions are crisp and fluid. Tone of voice across the interface is formal yet welcoming, prioritizing clear legibility, member trust, and celebratory prestige.

## Colors

The palette balances cool atmospheric slate tones with crisp daylight whites and curated warm amber accents.

- **Primary (`#4A6B82` - Dusty Slate Blue):** Anchors foundational structural elements: primary action buttons, key brand headers, high-importance tabs, and dominant navigation nodes. Communicates institutional permanence and dependability.
- **Secondary (`#8FA9BC` - Muted Icy Blue):** Serves transitional states, secondary interactive controls, passive progress indicators, soft fills, and subtle badge containers.
- **Tertiary (`#D4A359` - Polished Warm Gold):** Reserved intentionally for competitive distinction: medals, podium ranks, distinction ribbons, certified qualifications, and honors. Must never be used for generic alerts or standard buttons.
- **Neutral (`#5A6570` - Balanced Cool Slate):** Controls the tonal ladder across surfaces and text hierarchies.
  - Background Canvas: `#F4F7F9` (Pale Icy Slate).
  - Surface Containers: `#FFFFFF` (Pure Bright White) for raised content cards, stacked over `#EAF0F4` for recessed wells.
  - Typography: `#1E2832` for primary high-contrast titles, `#4D5C68` for secondary body copy, and `#788896` for tertiary metadata.
  - Borders & Dividers: `#DCE4EC` provides hairline structural containment without visual heaviness.

## Typography

The type system is powered entirely by **Hanken Grotesk**. Its balanced geometry, contemporary proportions, and crisp terminals convey technological precision alongside civic warmth.

- **Headlines & Display:** Set in Medium and Semi-Bold weights (`500` and `600`) with tight tracking to evoke architectural authority without feeling heavy or aggressive.
- **Body Text:** Renders in Regular (`400`) weight with generous line-heights for legal documentation, voting manifests, union bylaws, and competition rules.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Utilize Medium and Bold (`600` and `700`) weights with subtle positive letter spacing (`0.01em` to `0.04em`) to maintain sharp legibility in uppercase badges, standings tables, score markers, and form field tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The portal is built around a **12-column responsive fluid grid** with a maximum content constraint of `1440px`.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, and `3rem` (48px) exterior page margins. Broad side rails and calm margins reinforce an airy, uncluttered feel.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `1.25rem` (20px) gutters, and `2rem` (32px) margins.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` (16px) gutters, and `1.25rem` (20px) margins. Lateral content cards switch from multi-column tiles to vertically stacked, swipeable streams.

Component padding adheres strictly to the 4px baseline rhythm (`space-2xs` through `space-3xl`), prioritizing generous breathing room around interactive elements and official data tables.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in the design system relies on **tonal surface stacking** combined with **diffused, low-opacity ambient shadows** tinted with slate blue. It avoids heavy dark drops in favor of luminous atmospheric layers.

- **Layer 0 (Canvas Base):** `#F4F7F9` (Pale Icy Slate). Ground plane for the entire viewport.
- **Layer 1 (Standard Card / Panel):** `#FFFFFF` paired with a crisp hairline stroke (`1px solid #DCE4EC`) and subtle ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(74, 107, 130, 0.05), 0 4px 12px rgba(74, 107, 130, 0.03)`.
- **Layer 2 (Floating Dropdown / Sticky Rail):** `#FFFFFF` with `0 4px 16px rgba(74, 107, 130, 0.08), 0 1px 2px rgba(74, 107, 130, 0.04)`.
- **Layer 3 (Modals / Focus Overlays):** `#FFFFFF` with `0 16px 36px rgba(30, 40, 50, 0.12), 0 2px 6px rgba(74, 107, 130, 0.06)`, framed against a frosted glass backdrop filter (`backdrop-blur(8px)` with `rgba(244, 247, 249, 0.7)`).
- **Gold Merit State:** Active podium rankings and gold award badges receive a delicate luminous glow: `0 2px 10px rgba(212, 163, 89, 0.22)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Soft (`1`) shape profile**. The geometry is grounded, calm, and architectural rather than bubbly or toy-like.

- **Standard Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Badges, Tabs):** `0.25rem` (4px) border radius.
- **Containers & Surface Cards (`rounded-lg`):** `0.5rem` (8px) radius to maintain structured alignment across complex layouts.
- **Modals & Flyouts (`rounded-xl`):** `0.75rem` (12px) radius.
- **Special Indicators:** Circular pill radii (`9999px`) are restricted exclusively to numeric notification counts, avatar frames, and live competition status dots.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled Dusty Slate (`#4A6B82`) with pure white text. Hover transitions to `#3D5B70`. Padding: `0.625rem 1.25rem`. Radius: `4px`.
- **Secondary:** Surface white background with `#4A6B82` text and a `1px` border of `#CBD8E2`. Hover shifts background to `#F0F4F8`.
- **Podium / Distinction Action:** Reserved for award ceremonies and winner certification. Filled Warm Amber (`#D4A359`) with `#FFFFFF` text. Subtle warm hover glow (`#C49347`).
- **Ghost:** Transparent background with `#4D5C68` text; hover reveals an icy tint (`rgba(74, 107, 130, 0.06)`).

### Cards & Surfaces
- Clean white surfaces (`#FFFFFF`) framed with a delicate `1px solid #DCE4EC` perimeter border. Header areas within cards utilize a muted icy blue divider or an optional soft tint (`#FAFCFD`).
- Hoverable competition cards lift slightly via `translateY(-2px)` and shadow expansion to `0 8px 24px rgba(74, 107, 130, 0.08)`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- Height `40px`, crisp white background, border `1px solid #D0DCE5`, text in `#1E2832`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#4A6B82` alongside a `3px` focus ring in `rgba(74, 107, 130, 0.15)`. No stark black outlines.
- Helper and placeholder text: `#788896`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges:** Small (`22px` height), `4px` radius, uppercase tracking (`0.04em`).
- **Union Division / Committee:** Muted slate fill (`#EAF0F4`), text in `#4A6B82`.
- **Gold Honors / 1st Place:** Soft cream-gold fill (`rgba(212, 163, 89, 0.12)`), text in `#A37831`, border in `rgba(212, 163, 89, 0.28)`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size `18px x 18px`, `3px` radius for checkboxes, full circle for radios.
- Unchecked: `1px solid #CBD8E2` with a `#FFFFFF` fill.
- Checked: `#4A6B82` fill with a sharp white checkmark or center pip.

### Lists & Tables
- Border-collapsed rows separated by hairline `#EBF1F5` horizontal lines. Alternating hover rows highlight in `#F7FAFC`. Numeric competition data, union roll calls, and scores use tabular numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).

### Specialized Portal Components
- **Podium Display Tile:** Tiered vertical column cards (1st, 2nd, 3rd) featuring a top accent strip: 1st Place receives `#D4A359` (Gold), 2nd Place `#9EACB7` (Silver/Slate), and 3rd Place `#C89978` (Bronze).
- **Union Ballot / Voting Bar:** High-contrast binary voting meter showing proportional blue slate progress against a neutral ice-gray track.
